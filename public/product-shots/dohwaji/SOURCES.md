`seoul-day-1.webp`, `seoul-day-2.webp`, and `seoul-day-3.webp` show the corresponding day selected in the public map preview. The map region was cropped from browser screenshots and encoded as WebP. The landing page shows selected highlights of each day, not the full list of places. Its day buttons switch screenshots; the linked public service provides the actual interactive map.
